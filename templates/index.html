How to use VoiceFlow

1

Normal Dictation (Alt + Shift): Hold your Dictation Hotkey anywhere to speak. Release the keys to let VoiceFlow automatically paste the text into your active app. Try saying things like "new paragraph" or "comma" for flawless voice formatting!

2

Smart Context-Aware Replies (Ctrl + Shift): Copy any text (like an email or message) to your clipboard, then hold this hotkey to dictate your response. The AI will intelligently format your dictation as a polite, professional reply to what you copied. You can fully customize how the AI replies in the AI & Privacy settings!

3

AI Highlight Rewrite (Custom Presets): Highlight any messy text on your screen and press a custom preset hotkey (e.g., Ctrl + Space) to instantly rewrite and fix it in place. Add as many custom presets as you'd like in the settings!

4

Dictation Output Mode: If Auto-Type isn't pasting correctly in certain apps, go to Settings and change your output mode to "Copy to Clipboard Only". Then simply press Ctrl+V to paste your dictation perfectly every time.

5

Smart Filler Word Removal: Enable this in settings to have the AI magically scrub out all the "ums", "uhs", and stutters from your dictation before typing it out.

Your Privacy is Guaranteed

All of your audio is processed securely on your own system. The core dictation and transcription engine runs entirely locally, meaning your voice recordings and audio files never leave your device.

When you use the AI feature with Groq, only the specific text you highlight is sent to the Groq API for correction. If you require absolute zero-trust privacy, you can switch to Offline Mode (Local AI) and use Ollama (a completely open-source local model). With Ollama enabled, 100% of your data stays on your machine and nothing is ever sent to the cloud!
